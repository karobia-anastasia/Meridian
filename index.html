01 About Meridian Labs

Practical technology
for shared spaces.

We don’t just hand over software. We set it up, train your team and stay on hand, so moving to new tools is smooth and stress-free.

Meridian Labs brings together a family of digital products and professional services for places that bring people in: sports clubs, apartments and commercial buildings. From the front gate to the front desk to the point of sale, we help teams handle arrivals, bookings, sales and property requests with less paperwork and less guesswork.

We know that no two properties run the same way. That is why every engagement starts with understanding how your people work today. Some clients need one ready-made product. Others need several tools working together, or something built specifically for them. Either way, we stay involved from the first conversation to rollout and beyond.

What we believe

  • Support is part of the product. Setup, training and ongoing help come as standard, not as an extra.
  • No hassle for your team. We handle the technical work so your staff can focus on members, residents and customers.
  • Simple beats complicated. Tools should be easy for staff, residents and visitors to pick up on day one.
  • Fit the business, not the other way round. We adapt our products to your workflows and your space.
  • Grow at your own pace. Start with one product and add more as your needs change.
