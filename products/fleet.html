FLEET MANAGEMENT

Less guesswork.
Lower running costs.

When vehicle records live in notebooks and spreadsheets, it is hard to know what each vehicle costs, where it has been or when it is due for service. Our fleet management system brings vehicles, drivers, trips, fuel and maintenance together, so you can make decisions with clear numbers.

WHAT IT HELPS WITH

Run a tighter fleet,
day after day.

01 / VEHICLES

Every vehicle in one place

Keep registration, insurance, licences and documents for each vehicle organised, with reminders before anything expires.

02 / TRIPS

Plan and log trips

Assign vehicles and drivers to trips and keep a clear record of where each vehicle went and when.

03 / DRIVERS

Manage your drivers

Track driver assignments, licences and performance so the right person is behind every wheel.

04 / FUEL

Control fuel costs

Record fuel purchases per vehicle and spot unusual consumption before it eats into your margins.

05 / SERVICE

Stay ahead of maintenance

Schedule servicing by date or mileage and keep a full repair history, so vehicles spend less time off the road.

06 / REPORTS

Know your costs

See running costs per vehicle, trip and driver, with reports you can share with management.
