01 / Visitor management system

Welcome visitors.
Without the paper trail.

Portico is a secure visitor management system for banks, government offices, corporate buildings, clubs and apartments in Kenya. Hosts invite guests ahead, guards check them in on a tablet, and every visit leaves a full audit trail.

WHY PORTICO Smart access

No gate book.
No IDs at the gate.

Paper visitor books are hard to read, easy to fake and impossible to search when something goes wrong. Holding visitors’ IDs at the gate creates queues and puts personal data at risk.

Portico gives every visit a digital record, from invitation to check-out. Hosts know when their guests arrive, guards know who is expected, and managers get a searchable history of every visit.

HOW IT WORKS

From invite to check-out
in four steps.

01 / INVITE

Host sends an invite

Members, tenants or staff invite guests from the web. The guest receives a pass with a QR code by SMS.

02 / ARRIVE

Guard checks them in

The guard scans the pass on a tablet. Walk-ins are sent to the host to approve or decline from their phone.

03 / ACCESS

Right place, right time

Each visit is limited to the areas and times the host chose, such as the clubhouse but not the gym.

04 / CHECK OUT

Scan out at exit

Passes are scanned out when visitors leave. Overstays are flagged and you see who is still on site.

HIGH-SECURITY SITES Banks · Government · Regulated institutions

Visitor management for banks
and government offices.

Financial institutions, regulators and government agencies need more than a guest list. Portico gives security teams verified identities, strict zone control and records that stand up to audit.

01 / IDENTITY

Verified visitor identity

ID and licence scanning, photo capture and a phone number confirmed by one-time code, so guards know exactly who is entering.

02 / WATCHLIST

Watchlists and alerts

Flag barred or high-risk visitors at institution, site and host level, with instant alerts to the security team at the gate.

03 / ZONES

Restricted-area control

Limit each visitor to approved zones and time windows, require escorts for sensitive areas and block re-entry without exit.

04 / AUDIT

Complete audit trail

Every invitation, scan, approval and admin action is logged, with role-based access and exportable reports for compliance reviews.

05 / HOSTING

On-premise or cloud

Run Portico in our cloud or on your own servers, so visitor data never leaves your infrastructure.

06 / RESILIENCE

Built for continuity

Gate devices work offline during outages, and live on-site lists support evacuation roll calls and incident response.

DATA PROTECTION

Visitor data,
handled responsibly.

Portico is designed around Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019. Visitors see a consent notice at check-in, you choose how long records are kept, data is encrypted, and every admin and guard action is logged.

FAQ

Questions about
Portico.

Is Portico suitable for banks and government institutions?

Yes. Portico supports verified visitor identity, watchlists, restricted-zone control, escort rules, role-based access and complete audit logs, which are the controls banks, regulators and government agencies expect.

Can Portico be hosted on our own servers?

Yes. Portico can run in our cloud or be installed on-premise on your own infrastructure, so visitor data stays within your organisation.

What is Portico?

Portico is a visitor management system for sports clubs, apartments and commercial buildings in Kenya. It replaces the paper gate book with digital invitations, visitor passes and a guard app, so you always know who is on site.

Do visitors still need to leave their ID at the gate?

No. Each visitor gets a digital pass that is checked in at entry and checked out at exit. Visits that are not checked out are flagged, which does the job the ID deposit used to do.

What happens when the internet goes down at the gate?

The guard app keeps working offline. Expected visitors are stored on the device, check-ins are saved locally and everything syncs once the connection is back.

How does a host approve a walk-in visitor?

When an uninvited visitor names a host, the host gets a request on their phone to approve or decline. If the host does not respond, the request follows the escalation steps you set.

Can Portico check guests against our member or tenant list?

Yes. Portico can connect to your member or tenant records, so only active members or tenants can invite guests and club rules such as guest limits can be applied.

How is visitor data protected?

Portico is designed around Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019, with consent at check-in, configurable data retention, encrypted storage and a full log of who accessed what.
